One assignment that I found especially valuable was Question 2 from Problem Set 1. Early in the semester I was still figuring out what the expectations were for this class in terms of how detailed our solutions should be and how our work should be presented. Working through that problem helped me get a much better sense of the level of organization and clarity that is expected in the course. It showed me that the goal is not just to arrive at the final numerical answer, but to clearly lay out the assumptions, reasoning, and steps that lead to that answer.

It was also the first time I used the solution template provided for the course, which ended up being really helpful. Having sections for things like known values, assumptions, and analysis made it easier to structure my work and keep track of what I was doing. Before that, I tended to write solutions more informally, but the template encouraged a more systematic approach that made my work clearer and easier to follow. Overall, that assignment helped me understand how the workload and expectations in this class are structured, and it set a good foundation for how I approached later problem sets.
